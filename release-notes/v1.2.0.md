Context Hub updates itself now.

- It checks for a newer release a couple of seconds after launch. When one exists, a small banner offers **Update and restart**, which looks after itself: download with progress, then the installer runs and the app comes back on the new version.
- **Check for updates** in the library footer asks on demand.
- **Later** hides the banner until the next launch.

The first build with this in place is 1.2.0, so 1.2.0 and later update themselves. Anything older still needs a manual download, once.
